MODEL · KF-001
Compact Filter KF
Belt-fleece filtration system using gravity flow and continuous belt regeneration. Optimized for metalworking coolant (KSS) cleaning with high and low pressure supply pumps.
Filtration
01Dirty liquid flows laterally through intake box into filter trough
02Filter fleece retains dirt particles during flow-through
03Dirt particles form a filter cake — separates finest particles
04Clean liquid collects in clean tank
05Pumps supply machine tool with cleaned KSS as required
Regeneration
01Growing filter cake increases flow resistance
02Liquid level in filter trough rises
03Belt drive activates at defined level (or time-controlled)
04Carrier belt transports clean fleece onto filter surface
05Liquid level decreases — sludge container collects dirty fleece

MODEL · MP-003
Superfine Filter MicroPur®
Centrifugal superfine filtration with tangential inlet and cartridge filter elements. Automated pressure-differential regeneration with sludge tank and optional concentrator.
Filtration
01Filter pump conveys dirty coolant tangentially into filter bowl
02Centrifugal force accumulates larger solids on housing wall
03Coolant flows through filter elements outside → inside
04Filter cake forms on element surface — acts as deep-bed filter
05Clean filtrate enters clean tank
Regeneration
01Filter cake reduces flow or increases differential pressure
02Pressure/time control starts regeneration — intake closes, drain opens
03Flushing pump reverses flow inside → outside through element
04Filter cake released into sludge tank
05Automatic concentrator reduces sludge volume

Rotating drum vacuum filter with automatic back-wash regeneration and scraper belt for sludge removal. Self-contained system with high/low pressure pump supply.

01Contaminated liquid flows through intake box into filter
